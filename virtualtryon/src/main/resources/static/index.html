Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the WearFashion studio.

How does the AI virtual try-on work?

Our backend system utilizes advanced diffusion and synthesis models. It maps a garment photo onto a model's portrait, adjusting for lighting, sizing constraints, and natural fabric folds.

How do I upload custom models?

Under the Wardrobe tab, navigate to "Models" and click the upload zone. Drop a clean front-facing full portrait photo to create a persona.

Are my uploaded images secure?

Yes. All uploaded garments, personas, and generated assets are securely hosted locally or on private cloud storage, restricted to your user session ID.
